Perceptions of AI in Software Engineering: An Exploratory Study of Usefulness and Morality Among Students
Supervisor: Dr. Bianca Trinkenreich
  • Conducted a qualitative study utilizing semi-structured interviews and reflexive thematic analysis to investigate perceived usefulness and moral implications of AI tools among software engineering students.
  • Analyzed participant data to generate inductive codes, identifying key themes such as conditional trust, privacy concerns, and preference for AI as an assistive tool.

Balancing Compute Power and Fault Tolerance in Space Systems
Supervisor: Dr. Yashwant Malaiya
  • Analyzed modern fault tolerance in space systems, focusing on commercial FPGAs/GPUs and software-based fault correction with AI.
  • Programmed two panels to fold when ambient light is absent (such as during night cycles).
  • Synthesized viability, performance limits, and future adaptive directions for high-performance computing in space.

Color Harmonization in OST AR Devices: Enhancing Visibility of Virtual Objects in Bright Outdoor Environments
Supervisor: Mohammed Safayet Arefin
  • Engineered a background-aware color harmonization algorithm for optical see-through AR deployed via Unity on a Magic Leap 2 headset.

Speech-Guided Control for Autonomous Driving (Conformer-LSTM Part)
Supervisor: Nikhil Krishnaswamy
  • Developed a hybrid Conformer-LSTM Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) model using SpeechBrain and PyTorch with modular YAML configs.
  • Attained 7.83% Word Error Rate (WER) on LibriSpeech via dynamic batching and a joint CTC/KL Divergence loss function on a 144D architecture.

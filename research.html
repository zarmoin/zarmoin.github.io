Out of Focus Correction for Visual Impaired People
Ongoing
  • Extended reality (XR) devices often require users with refractive vision errors to rely on physical corrective lenses or add-on optical hardware, both of which introduce physical discomfort, extra weight, and increased device bulk. To overcome these hardware constraints, this research presents a software-driven vision compensation framework utilizing the SharpView image precorrection algorithm. By modeling individual ocular aberrations and applying constrained total-variation deconvolution, the method counteracts retinal defocus directly on rendered assets. This approach mitigates perceived image blur and significantly improves visual clarity, providing a comfortable, glasses-free XR experience.
